# Trademark & Logo FAQ

## Q1: Can I say my software “works with KERI Suite”?
Yes, as plain text, as long as it is accurate and does not imply endorsement.

## Q2: Can I use the logo on my website?
Yes, only to truthfully reference usage or compatibility.  
No, if it appears as your own branding or suggests partnership.

## Q3: Can I recolor or slightly tweak the logo?
No.  
Changing color, shape, proportions, or style is not allowed—even small changes.

## Q4: Can I design a logo that is “inspired by” yours?
No, if it is **confusingly similar**.  
“Inspired by” logos that resemble ours undermine brand clarity and are not permitted.

## Q5: Can I include the logo in my product UI?
No, not as a primary or decorative element.  
Possibly as an informational reference, with permission.

## Q6: Can I use the name or logo in my company or product name?
No.  
This includes domain names, app names, repositories, and social handles.

## Q7: Do I need permission for commercial use?
Yes.  
Any commercial, promotional, or branded use requires written approval.

## Q8: Why is this strict if the software is open source?
Because trademarks exist to protect users from confusion and misrepresentation.  
Open source protects freedom of code—not brand identity.

## Q9: What happens if I misuse the logo?
We will usually start with a friendly request to fix the issue.  
Continued misuse may lead to formal enforcement steps.

## Q10: I’m unsure—what should I do?
Contact **ssmith@keri.foundation** before publishing.
