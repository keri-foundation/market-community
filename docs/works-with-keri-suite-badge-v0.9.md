# “Works with KERI Suite” Badge Policy

## Purpose
The “Works with KERI Suite” badge allows third parties to communicate compatibility without using or modifying the KERI Suite logo.

## 1. What the Badge Means
The badge indicates that a product or service:
- Integrates with or supports KERI Suite
- Is not officially affiliated, certified, or endorsed

## 2. Badge Design Rules
- Badge design is provided by KERI Suite
- Text must read: “Works with KERI Suite”
- Colors, layout, and typography may not be altered
- No KERI Suite logo or icon is embedded in the badge

## 3. Where the Badge May Be Used

**Allowed**
- Product pages
- Documentation
- Integration listings
- Blog posts describing compatibility

**Not Allowed**
- Primary branding
- App icons or splash screens
- Merchandise
- Any usage suggesting certification or partnership

## 4. Disclaimer Requirement
The following disclaimer must appear near the badge:

> “This product is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by KERI Suite.”


## 5. Prohibited Uses
You may not:
- Modify the badge
- Combine the badge with your logo as a single mark
- Use the badge for misleading or incompatible products
- Continue use after permission has been revoked

## 6. Permission & Revocation
- The badge may be used without prior approval if all rules are followed
- KERI Suite reserves the right to request removal at any time
