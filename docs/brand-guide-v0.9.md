# KERI Suite – Logo Usage Guide

## ✅ DO
- Use only official logo files provided by KERI Suite
- Keep colors, proportions, and orientation unchanged
- Use the logo only to truthfully reference KERI Suite
- Leave sufficient clear space around the logo
- Use plain text when a logo is not necessary

## ❌ DON’T
- Do not recolor the logo
- Do not stretch, rotate, crop, or distort
- Do not add effects (shadows, outlines, gradients, animations)
- Do not combine with other logos or text into one mark
- Do not redraw, simplify, or stylize the logo
- Do not create “inspired by” or look-alike logos

> If it looks like “almost the KERI Suite logo”, it is not allowed.

## ❌ NEVER USE THE LOGO AS
- Your company or product logo
- An app icon or favicon
- A primary UI branding element
- A certification or endorsement mark

## ✔ TEXT-ONLY REFERENCES (OK)
- “Compatible with KERI Suite”
- “Built on KERI Suite”
- “Uses the KERI Suite open-source software”

Add a disclaimer where appropriate:

> “This product is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by KERI Suite.”
