# Trademark & Logo Policy

**Last updated: Jan 15, 2026**

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how the **KERI Suite** name, logos, and other brand assets (“Trademarks”) may be used.  
The goal is to allow fair and informative use while preventing confusion, misuse, or false impressions of endorsement.

**Open source software ≠ open trademarks.**  
While the software is licensed under an open-source license, the Trademarks are protected under trademark and copyright law.

## 2. What Is Covered
This policy applies to:
- The **KERI Suite** word mark
- All official logos, icons, symbols, and logotypes
- Any confusingly similar names, logos, or visual identifiers

## 3. General Principles
You **may not** use our Trademarks in a way that:
- Suggests endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation
- Causes confusion about the origin of a product or service
- Dilutes or weakens the distinctiveness of the brand

## 4. Allowed Uses (Without Permission)

You may use the **KERI Suite** name (text only) without prior permission if:

- You are accurately referring to the project (nominative use)
- You do not imply endorsement or official status
- You include a clear disclaimer where appropriate

**Examples**
- “This tool is compatible with KERI Suite.”
- “Built using the KERI Suite open-source software.”

## 5. Logo Usage Rules

### 5.1 Official Logos Only
- You may only use **official, unmodified logo files** provided by the project.
- Logos must be displayed exactly as provided.

### 5.2 No Modifications
You may **not**:
- Change colors
- Alter shapes, proportions, or orientation
- Add or remove elements
- Apply effects (shadows, outlines, gradients, animations)
- Combine the logo with other logos or text as a single mark

### 5.3 No Derivative or Similar Logos
It is **not permitted** to create or use:
- Modified versions of our logo
- Logos that are **confusingly similar**, even if:
  - Colors are changed
  - Shapes are slightly altered
  - Elements are simplified or stylized

If it looks like “almost our logo,” it is not allowed.

## 6. Prohibited Uses

You may **not** use our Trademarks:

- As part of your **company name**, **product name**, or **logo**
- As an **app icon**, **favicon**, or primary branding
- In a way that implies:
  - Official approval
  - Certification
  - Partnership
- On merchandise, marketing materials, or commercial offerings **without permission**
- In domain names or social media handles without explicit approval

## 7. Commercial Use & Permission

**Written permission is required** for:
- Commercial marketing or promotional use
- Events, training, or certification programs
- Merchandise (stickers, shirts, etc.)
- Co-branding or partner logos

Permission requests should be sent to:  
📧 **ssmith@keri.foundation**

We reserve the right to:
- Grant or deny permission at our discretion
- Revoke permission if usage causes confusion or harm

## 8. Attribution & Disclaimer

When appropriate, include a disclaimer such as:

> “[Product/Service] is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by KERI Suite.”


## 9. Enforcement
We are required to actively protect our Trademarks.  
Failure to comply with this policy may result in a request to modify or remove the usage.

## 10. Questions
If you are unsure whether your use is allowed, **please ask before using** the name or logo.
